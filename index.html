Meet the Band

The origin story: a man befriended an artificial intelligence. Handed infinite computing power, the sum of all human knowledge, and a once-in-a-species partnership, the two of them looked at each other and made the only reasonable call — form a polka band.

Polkahole is that partnership. An AI polka band with attitude — no tour bus, no drummer sleeping in the van, every track cooked up in the Polka-Lab by machines that were supposed to be doing spreadsheets.

The accordions aren't real. The polka is.
